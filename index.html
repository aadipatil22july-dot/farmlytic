Plan a field from area and inputs to yield, revenue and risk — not just calculate one number.

🗓️ Field Planning Workflow

01 · Field Record area, soil condition, water availability and previous crop.
02 · Planting Select crop/variety, seed rate, spacing and target plant population.
03 · Inputs Budget seed, fertilizer, crop protection, labour, machinery and irrigation.
04 · Crop care Track irrigation, scouting, nutrient operations and field observations.
05 · Harvest Record harvested quantity, marketable quantity and post-harvest losses.
06 · Economics Compare revenue, profit, B:C and break-even values.

📋 Field Record Checklist

  • Field area and land unit
  • Crop and variety
  • Sowing/transplanting date
  • Seed quantity and seed treatment
  • Fertilizer/nutrient applications
  • Irrigation dates and approximate quantity
  • Pest/disease observations and treatments
  • Labour and machinery hours
  • Harvest and sale records

Keeping these records turns Farmlytic from a calculator into a simple farm decision tool.

Farm economics

Farm budget in one workflow

1. Enter field area → 2. calculate inputs and cost → 3. estimate marketable yield → 4. enter selling price → 5. calculate revenue and profit → 6. check B:C and break-even.

Key formulas: Revenue = Yield × Price · Profit = Revenue − Total Cost · B:C = Benefit ÷ Cost · Break-even price = Cost ÷ Marketable Yield.

Use your own field records and realistic local price assumptions. Costs, yields and prices can change.
